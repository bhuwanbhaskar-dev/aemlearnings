I debugged the pipeline build failure log and identified the root cause:
Issue:
WordDelimiterFilterFactory was failing because preserveOriginal="{Boolean}true" was being passed as a boolean. The filter expects an integer value (1 or 0), causing NumberFormatException and blocking the deployment.
Fix:
Changed to:
XML<WordDelimiter jcr:primaryType="nt:unstructured" preserveOriginal="1"/>
I have applied the fix locally and triggered a new deployment pipeline. I’ve also updated Adobe Support ticket E-001882803 with the details and the official Javadoc reference for their review.
